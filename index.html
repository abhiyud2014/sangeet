01 — The evening

An evening of song,
held in the light.

On the evening of Maha Astami, under a marigold-lit stage, voices rose one after another — stotram and bhajan, a Geeta path, and old songs carried by harmonium and tabla. Sangeetayana brought a neighbourhood together around its music, and the lamps stayed on until a little past eight.

These are the frames between the notes: the wait before a performance, the folded hands after one, the quiet faces in the blue chairs.
